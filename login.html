How accounts work here This is a static site with no backend. Local passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (210,000 iterations) and a unique salt before being stored in this browser. Sessions expire after 12 hours. Data never leaves this device.

Google sign-in is handled by Google Identity Services. This static build reads the returned ID token in the browser and checks its audience and expiry; it cannot verify the token's signature without a server. Use it for personal, non-sensitive tracking only.
